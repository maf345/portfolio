## Problem

Healthcare and commercial stakeholders needed faster, more reliable answers from complex analytical datasets — without sacrificing accuracy on medical and business logic.

## Approach

- Led delivery across developers, QA, BA, Scrum Master, and product stakeholders.
- Translated business problems into analytical requirements and validated structured outputs against business rules.
- Designed Agentic AI and LLM-assisted workflows with emphasis on prompt quality and evaluation via LangSmith.
- Owned complex tickets end-to-end: root cause analysis, dependency resolution, and release validation.

## Stack

SQL · Snowflake · Python · LangSmith · Tableau · Agile delivery workflows

## Outcome

Improved alignment between data-driven outputs and stakeholder needs while maintaining KPI accuracy through reconciliation, anomaly checks, and traceability analysis.
