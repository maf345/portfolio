## Problem

Operations teams needed a reliable way to track customer bandwidth upgrades and downgrades — with historical logs, aggregated summaries, and commission tracking for billing workflows.

## Approach

- Developed a Laravel + MySQL application with role-based pages for links, clients, and change logs.
- Built daywise change logs plus day-end and month-end aggregated summary views.
- Added forms for bandwidth commission tracking and new link creation.

## Stack

PHP · Laravel · MySQL · HTML · CSS · JavaScript

## Outcome

Centralized bandwidth history and reporting — reducing manual lookup and supporting operational and billing teams with structured audit trails.
